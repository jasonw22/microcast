Breakdowns of the backtest: where, when and how the models beat raw HRRR.

Skill by lead time

Forecasts are issued 55 minutes after the HRRR cycle, so a 1 h lead is a short nowcast and 6 h is about five hours ahead.

Skill by station

Dot = skill vs raw HRRR, whisker = 95% day-block bootstrap interval. Stations come from the configured registry and available observations.

Error by time of day

Mean absolute error of the forecast mean, by valid hour (Pacific time). The marine layer and the afternoon sea breeze both show up here.

Calibration

PIT histograms: where each observation fell in its forecast distribution. Flat at 10% is calibrated; a U shape means overconfident, a hump means too cautious, a slope means biased.

Where HRRR goes wrong

Raw HRRR's average error by month and hour of day. This is the pattern the models learn to remove.
